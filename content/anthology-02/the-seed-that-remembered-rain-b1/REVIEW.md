## Cenas e perguntas

### A Sound in the Nursery (`silent-nursery`)

Lena worked in a nursery beneath the city’s glass dome. Rain had been absent for so long that its sound survived mainly in recordings. Most of her seeds remained dormant, despite carefully controlled water and light. One evening, while she was cataloguing an amber data cartridge, a recording of rainfall played through the desk speaker. "That tray was still this morning. Something has changed." A single pale shoot was lifting from the nearest pot. The water setting had not changed.

**Pergunta:** Why does Lena notice the shoot?

- **a** — It grows outside the glass dome
- **b** — It appears although she has not changed the water setting
- **c** — It is the first seed she has watered that day

**Gabarito editorial:** b. The shoot appears while the controlled water setting remains unchanged.

**Dicas:** nursery → viveiro de plantas; dormant → dormentes; sem germinar.

### Changing One Thing (`careful-test`)

The next morning, Lena repeated the test with Arun. They kept the lamps, temperature and water unchanged, then played the same rainfall recording. The small shoot leaned towards the silver speaker. When the recording stopped, it slowly straightened. A recording of traffic produced no visible response. "It is a pattern worth testing, not an explanation yet." Lena agreed. She labelled the tray for observation and saved a copy of the sound, refusing to turn one unusual result into a claim about every seed.

**Pergunta:** What can the researchers reasonably conclude at this point?

- **a** — The observed response deserves further testing
- **b** — Every seed needs the sound of rain
- **c** — Traffic prevents all plants from growing

**Gabarito editorial:** a. They have observed a repeatable response in one shoot, but do not yet have a general explanation.

**Dicas:** straightened → voltou à posição reta; claim → afirmação; conclusão declarada.

### What to Share First (`two-proposals`)

The city archive could preserve the rainfall recording together with the experiment notes. A neighbourhood greenhouse had also offered a controlled space for a second trial. Lena had enough time to prepare one delivery that afternoon. Sending the cartridge to the archive would protect its context; taking a copy to the greenhouse would let another team attempt the test. "Both matter. We must choose which conversation happens first." She placed the original amber cartridge beside a clean copy and kept the seed tray inside the nursery.

**Pergunta:** What is the difference between the two proposals?

- **a** — One changes the water; the other changes the lamps
- **b** — One destroys the cartridge; the other removes the dome
- **c** — One preserves the record; the other supports an independent trial

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The archive preserves the recording and notes, while the greenhouse can independently repeat the test.

**Dicas:** trial → ensaio; teste experimental; context → contexto; informações de origem.

- Preserve the original and its context in the archive → `archive-record`
- Share a copy for an independent greenhouse trial → `greenhouse-trial`

### More Than a Pleasant Sound (`archive-record`)

At the city archive, Lena deposited the original cartridge with her notes and kept the copy for future tests. The archivist found that the recording had been made in a garden which no longer existed. It included the soft rattle of rain against a greenhouse roof, not simply falling water. "Keep that detail with the sound. We do not know which part matters." Back at the nursery, the shoot was still small. Lena had not restored the rain or solved the experiment. She had made sure that the next person could ask a better question.

**Pergunta:** Why does Lena ask the archivist to keep the recording’s context?

- **a** — They do not yet know which part of the sound may matter
- **b** — The notes have proved that all rainfall sounds are identical
- **c** — She wants the archivist to replace the recording with music

**Gabarito editorial:** a. The particular roof sound may matter, so preserving context supports future investigation.

**Dicas:** rattle → ruído leve de batidas repetidas; restored → restabelecido.

Fim deste percurso.

### A Question Shared (`greenhouse-trial`)

Lena took the copied recording and experiment notes to the neighbourhood greenhouse, leaving the original safely at the nursery. The second team prepared matching trays and agreed to record failures as carefully as successes. Their first trial showed no clear movement, although the water and lamps matched Lena’s settings. "A result that differs from ours is still useful. Let us compare the details." They arranged another observation for the following day. Outside, the dome remained dry, but the sound of rain had become a question that more than one team could investigate.

**Pergunta:** How does Lena respond when the second trial shows no clear movement?

- **a** — She asks the team to hide the result
- **b** — She treats it as useful evidence and compares the details
- **c** — She announces that the city’s rain has returned

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Lena treats a different result as useful evidence rather than hiding it.

**Dicas:** matching → equivalentes; preparados nas mesmas condições; failures → resultados sem sucesso.

Fim deste percurso.
