# A Very Important Rehearsal — A1

História original assistida por IA para English Roulette. Um ator tenta fazer uma entrada majestosa, mas um pato de brinquedo transforma o ensaio em uma escolha entre solenidade e diversão.

**Gênero:** Comédia · **Nível proposto:** A1. Cinco cenas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. Nível editorial, sem certificação CEFR ou declaração de revisão humana.

Este documento contém gabaritos editoriais e deve permanecer fora de `wwwroot`. A narração publicada usa uma única voz sintética; os tempos de cada fala foram medidos no PCM. As imagens seguem desenho 2D dark, com cenas e elencos próprios.

## A Sound beneath the Crown (`royal-entrance`)

Ben stands on a small stage in a blue coat and a paper crown. He is the king in a play. He takes one big step. A loud sound comes from the floor. "That is not a king. That is a duck!"

**Pergunta:** What does Ben hear when he takes a step?

- **a** — A duck sound
- **b** — A doorbell
- **c** — A drum

**Gabarito editorial:** a. A duck sound. Evidence: That is a duck!

**Dicas:** crown → coroa; stage → palco.

- Continue the story → `small-singer`

## A Very Small Singer (`small-singer`)

Dara lifts a loose board near Ben's feet. A yellow toy duck is under it. The board pushes the duck down when Ben steps on it. "We found our very small singer." Ben laughs. The king's voice is fine.

**Pergunta:** Where is the yellow toy duck?

- **a** — Inside the paper crown
- **b** — Under a loose board
- **c** — Behind the curtain

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Under a loose board. Evidence: A yellow toy duck is under it.

**Dicas:** loose → solta; pushes → pressiona.

- Continue the story → `entrance-choice`

## Two Ways to Be a King (`entrance-choice`)

They can take the toy duck away. Ben can also keep it under the board and change his part in the play. "A duck king can be a good king, too." Dara waits. Ben must choose his entrance before the other actors arrive.

**Pergunta:** Who chooses how the king will enter?

- **a** — Dara
- **b** — The other actors
- **c** — Ben

**Gabarito editorial:** c. Ben. Evidence: Ben must choose his entrance

**Dicas:** away → para outro lugar; para longe; entrance → entrada em cena.

- Keep the duck and change the king → `duck-king`
- Remove the duck for a quiet entrance → `quiet-practice`

## Make Way for the Duck King (`duck-king`)

Ben puts the board back over the toy duck. He takes a slow step onto it. Ben hears the duck again. "Make way for the Duck King!" Dara laughs and claps. Ben bends forward. He is ready for the other actors.

**Pergunta:** Who laughs and claps for Ben?

- **a** — Dara
- **b** — The other actors
- **c** — A real duck

**Gabarito editorial:** a. Dara. Evidence: Dara laughs and claps.

**Dicas:** bends → inclina-se; claps → bate palmas.

Fim deste percurso.

## A Quiet Floor and a Happy King (`quiet-practice`)

Dara takes the toy duck away and puts it on a box. Ben walks across the stage. The floor is quiet now. "I miss my small singer." He makes a duck sound himself. Dara laughs. Their quiet practice ends with one very happy king.

**Pergunta:** Who makes the last duck sound?

- **a** — The toy under the board
- **b** — Ben himself
- **c** — Someone inside the box

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Ben himself. Evidence: He makes a duck sound himself.

**Dicas:** himself → ele mesmo; practice → ensaio; prática.

Fim deste percurso.
