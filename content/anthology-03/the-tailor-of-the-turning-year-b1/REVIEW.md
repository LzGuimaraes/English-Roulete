# The Tailor of the Turning Year — B1

História original assistida por IA para English Roulette. Uma alfaiate ajuda o guardião do inverno a deixar sua estação partir sem perder a lembrança do que amou nela.

**Gênero:** Fantasia · **Nível proposto:** B1. Cinco cenas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. Nível editorial, sem certificação CEFR ou declaração de revisão humana.

Este documento contém gabaritos editoriais e deve permanecer fora de `wwwroot`. A narração publicada usa uma única voz sintética; os tempos de cada fala foram medidos no PCM. As imagens seguem desenho 2D dark, com cenas e elencos próprios.

## The Last Thread of Winter (`unfinished-coat`)

Rin was the tailor who made a new coat for the keeper of each season. On the final evening of winter, she waited in her hilltop workshop with a nearly finished green coat for spring. Sev, the winter keeper, should have arrived before sunset to return his silver spool. Without that last thread, the coat's lining could not be closed. When the door finally opened, Sev held the spool tightly. "Please leave the coat unfinished. I am not ready to let winter go."

**Pergunta:** What is needed to finish the spring coat?

- **a** — The last thread from the silver spool
- **b** — A different green fabric
- **c** — A new wooden chair

**Gabarito editorial:** a. The last thread from the silver spool. Evidence: Without that last thread, the coat's lining could not be closed.

**Dicas:** lining → forro; spool → carretel.

- Continue the story → `a-keepers-regret`

## What the Spool Remembered (`a-keepers-regret`)

Sev explained that winter vanished from his mind whenever he gave up its final thread. He would remember his duties, but not the quiet pleasure of snow falling against a window. "This spool holds the last of that feeling." Rin had always believed that the keepers welcomed each new season without regret. Now she understood why he had arrived late. She set her needle down and offered him a chair. Finishing the coat mattered, but rushing him would not make the parting easier.

**Pergunta:** What is Sev afraid of losing?

- **a** — His job as a tailor
- **b** — The remembered feeling of winter
- **c** — The key to the workshop

**Gabarito editorial:** b. The remembered feeling of winter. Evidence: not the quiet pleasure of snow falling against a window

**Dicas:** regret → pesar; parting → despedida.

- Continue the story → `two-memories`

## A Memory to Carry (`two-memories`)

Rin examined the spool. There was enough thread to close the spring coat, with a little left over for Sev's own blue cloak. She knew two ways to keep a memory inside a garment. A hidden pocket could hold one real snowflake, unchanged until the pocket was opened again. A woven design could preserve the sound of snowfall, though it would contain no snow. Either would let Sev finish his work and remember what he loved. Rin explained both possibilities and let him choose.

**Pergunta:** What would a woven design preserve?

- **a** — One real snowflake
- **b** — The heat of summer
- **c** — The sound of snowfall

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The sound of snowfall. Evidence: A woven design could preserve the sound of snowfall

**Dicas:** garment → peça de roupa; woven → tecido; entrelaçado.

- Make a pocket for one real snowflake → `snowflake-pocket`
- Weave the sound of snowfall into the cloak → `snowfall-pattern`

## A Place for One Snowflake (`snowflake-pocket`)

Sev chose the pocket. Rin stitched it inside his blue cloak and held it open near the window. One snowflake drifted in and remained bright against the cloth. She used the rest of the thread to finish the green spring coat. Outside, the first buds appeared on bare branches. Sev fastened his cloak, feeling the tiny pocket against his chest. Winter no longer filled the room, but its memory could travel with him. "Now I can rest until winter returns." He stepped outside.

**Pergunta:** What remains inside the hidden pocket?

- **a** — One real snowflake
- **b** — The sound of a bell
- **c** — A broken needle

**Gabarito editorial:** a. One real snowflake. Evidence: One snowflake drifted in and remained bright against the cloth.

**Dicas:** stitched → costurou; buds → brotos.

Fim deste percurso.

## The Sound in the Lining (`snowfall-pattern`)

Sev chose the woven design. Rin used the extra thread to stitch a silver spiral inside his blue cloak. When he brushed it with his fingers, he heard snow settling softly on a windowsill. There was no ice against his skin, only the remembered sound. Rin finished the spring coat with the remaining thread. Outside, the first buds appeared. Sev closed his eyes once, then opened the door without hesitation. "I can welcome what comes next without pretending that winter meant nothing."

**Pergunta:** What happens when Sev touches the silver spiral?

- **a** — His skin turns into ice
- **b** — He hears the sound of snow settling
- **c** — The spring coat disappears

**Gabarito editorial:** b. He hears the sound of snow settling. Evidence: he heard snow settling softly on a windowsill

**Dicas:** windowsill → peitoril da janela; hesitation → hesitação.

Fim deste percurso.
