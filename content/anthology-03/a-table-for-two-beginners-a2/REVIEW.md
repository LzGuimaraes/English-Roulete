# A Table for Two Beginners — A2

História original assistida por IA para English Roulette. Duas pessoas se aproximam em uma aula de cerâmica e escolhem como transformar uma conversa desajeitada em um próximo encontro.

**Gênero:** Romance · **Nível proposto:** A2. Cinco cenas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. Nível editorial, sem certificação CEFR ou declaração de revisão humana.

## Two Unusual Bowls (`first-bowls`)

Rosa joined an evening pottery class after work. She chose a place beside a man in a blue apron. His bowl leaned to one side, and hers had a hole in the bottom. "Water can leave through that hole. Your bowl is ready for rain." "Then perhaps it is a flower pot." They laughed. The teacher asked everyone to leave their work to dry.

**Pergunta:** What is wrong with Rosa's bowl?

- **a** — It has a broken handle
- **b** — It is perfectly round
- **c** — It has a hole in the bottom

**Gabarito editorial:** c. It has a hole in the bottom. Evidence: hers had a hole in the bottom

**Dicas:** leaned → estava inclinada; apron → avental.

## A Different Kind of Plan (`cleaning-together`)

While they cleaned the table, Rosa found a small drawing on Eli's notebook. It showed a perfect round bowl. "That was the plan. Mine looks like a tired hat." Rosa showed him her own page. She had drawn a plant in a simple pot. "Perhaps we made the things we actually needed." Eli smiled and asked her name. They stayed to finish cleaning together.

**Pergunta:** What had Rosa drawn on her page?

- **a** — A plant in a simple pot
- **b** — A hat on a chair
- **c** — A perfectly round bowl

**Gabarito editorial:** a. A plant in a simple pot. Evidence: She had drawn a plant in a simple pot.

**Dicas:** round → redonda; actually → na verdade.

- Continue the story → `outside-studio`

## Finding the Words (`outside-studio`)

Outside the studio, Eli told Rosa that he wanted to see her again. "I enjoyed the class much more with you." Rosa felt the same, but she needed a moment to find the words. She could invite him for a short walk by the canal, or ask him to share a table at the next class. "I would like that, too."

**Pergunta:** How does Rosa feel about seeing Eli again?

- **a** — She wants to avoid him
- **b** — She wants to see him again too
- **c** — She only wants to borrow his notebook

**Gabarito editorial:** b. She wants to see him again too. Evidence: Rosa felt the same

**Dicas:** canal → canal; share → dividir; compartilhar.

- Invite Eli for a walk by the canal → `canal-walk`

## An Evening beyond the Studio (`canal-walk`)

Rosa asked Eli to walk beside the canal. He said yes. They talked about the things they hoped to make at the studio. At a small bridge, he stopped to watch the lights on the water. "Next time, I will bring a picture of my bowl." "Only if you let me bring my flower pot." They chose another evening to meet before they said goodbye.

**Pergunta:** What do Rosa and Eli decide before saying goodbye?

- **a** — To leave the pottery class forever
- **b** — To sell their bowls at the bridge
- **c** — Another evening to meet

**Gabarito editorial:** c. Another evening to meet. Evidence: They chose another evening to meet

**Dicas:** bridge → ponte; goodbye → despedida.

Fim deste percurso.

## The Seat beside Him (`next-saturday`)

Rosa asked Eli to share her table at the next class. A week later, he arrived early and saved the seat beside him. Their first bowls were dry on a shelf. "My flower pot needs a small plate under it." "And my tired hat needs a friend." They worked side by side, then made plans for coffee. This time, neither of them hurried home.

**Pergunta:** What does Eli save for Rosa?

- **a** — The seat beside him
- **b** — A place on the canal boat
- **c** — A new blue apron

**Gabarito editorial:** a. The seat beside him. Evidence: saved the seat beside him

**Dicas:** shelf → prateleira; hurried → foi embora com pressa.

Fim deste percurso.
