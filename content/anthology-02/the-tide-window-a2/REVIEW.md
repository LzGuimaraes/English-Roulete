# The Tide Window — A2

Conteúdo original criado e publicado a pedido do responsável em 08/11/2026. Cinco cenas físicas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. As escolhas são caminhos narrativos válidos. Duas traduções contextuais por cena; falas cobrem integralmente a narração, com tempos medidos no áudio produzido.

Nível proposto editorialmente, sem certificação CEFR ou alegação de revisão humana. Arte 2D ilustrada dark; sem pessoas reais. Gravação sintética única para narrador e personagens. Este documento contém gabaritos editoriais e deve permanecer fora do diretório público.

## Cenas e perguntas

### Seeds for the Island (`harbour-box`)

Bea arrived at the harbour with a sealed orange box of seeds. The island garden needed them for its spring opening. The morning sea was calm, and a marked stone path was beginning to appear as the tide fell. "The box must stay dry. These seeds have travelled a long way." She kept the box under her coat and went to the harbour office.

**Pergunta:** Why does Bea protect the orange box?

- **a** — The box contains a working radio
- **b** — The box belongs to the boat captain
- **c** — The seeds inside must stay dry

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The sealed box contains seeds that need to stay dry.

**Dicas:** sealed → fechado; lacrado; harbour → porto.

- Continue the story → `harbour-map`

### Two Ways Across (`harbour-map`)

Jon showed Bea the harbour map. The marked path would be open for another hour. The supply boat would leave in twenty minutes and return from the island later that morning. Both routes reached the garden before opening time. "The path gives you a quiet walk. The boat carries tools as well." Bea thanked him and checked that the orange box was still sealed.

**Pergunta:** What do both routes allow Bea to do?

- **a** — Return to the harbour before sunrise
- **b** — Reach the garden before opening time
- **c** — Avoid carrying the orange box

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Both the path and the supply boat arrive before the garden opens.

**Dicas:** supply boat → barco de suprimentos; as well → também.

- Continue the story → `choose-crossing`

### The Keeper’s Request (`choose-crossing`)

The garden keeper called the office radio. She had space ready for the seeds and needed a spare watering can. Bea could take the marked path now, or wait with Jon and carry the can on the boat. "Either way, the seeds will arrive. I must choose how to help." Outside, the sea remained calm. The orange box was light enough for either journey.

**Pergunta:** What extra item does the keeper ask for?

- **a** — A spare watering can
- **b** — A larger orange box
- **c** — A new harbour map

**Gabarito editorial:** a. The keeper asks for a spare watering can in addition to the seeds.

**Dicas:** keeper → responsável pelo jardim; spare → reserva; adicional.

- Take the marked path now → `stone-path`
- Wait and bring the can by boat → `supply-crossing`

### A Garden Before the Crowd (`stone-path`)

Bea followed the marked stone path and reached the garden while the keeper was arranging empty pots. Together, they put the orange box on a dry workbench. Jon would bring the watering can on the boat. "I have time to help you prepare the first row." They planted the seeds before the visitors arrived. Looking back across the calm water, Bea could see the blue boat leaving the harbour.

**Pergunta:** How will the watering can reach the island?

- **a** — Bea will carry it back along the path
- **b** — Jon will bring it on the supply boat
- **c** — The keeper will collect it after closing

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Jon is bringing the can by boat while Bea helps prepare the garden.

**Dicas:** workbench → bancada de trabalho; row → fileira.

Fim deste percurso.

### An Extra Pair of Hands (`supply-crossing`)

Bea waited with Jon, then travelled on the supply boat. She kept the orange box on her lap while Jon secured the watering can beside the tools. At the island, the keeper met them at the small landing. "We brought your watering can, and the seeds are still dry." Together, they carried everything into the garden. Bea stayed to help the keeper welcome the first visitors, glad that one journey had carried two kinds of help.

**Pergunta:** What does Bea do during the boat journey?

- **a** — She plants the seeds in the boat
- **b** — She opens the box beside the water
- **c** — She keeps the orange box on her lap

**Gabarito editorial:** c. Bea protects the seed box on her lap while Jon secures the watering can.

**Dicas:** lap → colo; landing → pequeno cais de desembarque.

Fim deste percurso.
