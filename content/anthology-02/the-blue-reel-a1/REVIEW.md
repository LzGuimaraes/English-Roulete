# The Blue Reel — A1

Conteúdo original criado e publicado a pedido do responsável em 08/11/2026. Cinco cenas físicas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. As escolhas são caminhos narrativos válidos. Duas traduções contextuais por cena; falas cobrem integralmente a narração, com tempos medidos no áudio produzido.

Nível proposto editorialmente, sem certificação CEFR ou alegação de revisão humana. Arte 2D ilustrada dark; sem pessoas reais. Gravação sintética única para narrador e personagens. Este documento contém gabaritos editoriais e deve permanecer fora do diretório público.

## Cenas e perguntas

### After the Last Film (`empty-cinema`)

Iris cleans a small cinema at night. Under a seat, she finds a blue film reel in a yellow bag. The bag is dry and clean. "Someone was here today. This is not old rubbish." She takes the bag to the projection room. A small white envelope is inside.

**Pergunta:** What does Iris find under a seat?

- **a** — A blue film reel in a yellow bag
- **b** — A white coat and a red ticket
- **c** — An empty box of sweets

**Gabarito editorial:** a. Iris finds a blue film reel inside a yellow bag under a seat.

**Dicas:** reel → bobina de filme; rubbish → lixo.

- Continue the story → `written-note`

### A Note for the Cinema (`written-note`)

In the projection room, Iris opens the envelope. The note says the film shows this cinema many years ago. There is no name on the note. "The old picture on this shelf may help me." In the picture, a young man holds a blue reel. Iris knows his face.

**Pergunta:** What does Iris learn from the note?

- **a** — The reel belongs in the rubbish
- **b** — The film is about a flower shop
- **c** — The film shows the cinema many years ago

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The note says the film shows this cinema many years ago. It does not name the sender.

**Dicas:** shelf → prateleira; many years ago → há muitos anos.

- Continue the story → `two-ways`

### The Man in the Picture (`two-ways`)

The man in the picture is Mr Hale. He has a flower shop near the cinema. Iris puts the blue reel beside the projector. The machine works. "I can watch the film, or take the bag to his shop." She has time for one visit before she goes home.

**Pergunta:** Where does Mr Hale work now?

- **a** — In the cinema kitchen
- **b** — At a flower shop near the cinema
- **c** — At a station far from town

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Mr Hale now has a flower shop near the cinema.

**Dicas:** projector → projetor; near → perto de.

- Watch the film in the cinema → `film-on-screen`
- Take the bag to Mr Hale → `flower-shop`

### The First Evening (`film-on-screen`)

Iris watches the film. On the screen, young Mr Hale opens the cinema doors for the first time. His family stands beside him. At the end, he waves. "Now I know why this little film is important." She keeps the reel safe. Tomorrow, she will invite him to watch it with her.

**Pergunta:** What does the film show?

- **a** — Mr Hale closing his flower shop
- **b** — Iris cleaning the projection room
- **c** — Mr Hale opening the cinema for the first time

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The film shows the cinema opening, with young Mr Hale and his family.

**Dicas:** waves → acena; safe → em segurança.

Fim deste percurso.

### A Present Returned (`flower-shop`)

Iris takes the yellow bag to the flower shop. Mr Hale looks at the blue reel and smiles. He left it at the cinema as a present. "I forgot to write my name. Thank you for finding me." Iris asks him to bring his family to the cinema. They can tell the story together.

**Pergunta:** Why did Mr Hale leave the reel at the cinema?

- **a** — He left it there as a present
- **b** — He wanted to throw it away
- **c** — He wanted Iris to sell it

**Gabarito editorial:** a. Mr Hale says he left the film as a present for the cinema.

**Dicas:** present → presente; forgot → esqueceu.

Fim deste percurso.
