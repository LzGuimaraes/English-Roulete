# The Voice in Carriage Nine — A2

Conteúdo original criado e publicado a pedido do responsável em 08/11/2026. Cinco cenas físicas, quatro por percurso e dois finais completos. As escolhas são caminhos narrativos válidos. Duas traduções contextuais por cena; falas cobrem integralmente a narração, com tempos medidos no áudio produzido.

Nível proposto editorialmente, sem certificação CEFR ou alegação de revisão humana. Arte 2D ilustrada dark; sem pessoas reais. Gravação sintética única para narrador e personagens. Este documento contém gabaritos editoriais e deve permanecer fora do diretório público.

## Cenas e perguntas

### One Coat Too Many (`last-inspection`)

Mara checked the empty train before the last lights went out. In carriage nine, a red raincoat hung over a seat. It looked exactly like hers, including the small repair on its sleeve. "My coat is in my locker. I left it there this morning." She lifted her yellow torch. Water was dripping from the coat onto a dry floor.

**Pergunta:** Which detail made Mara think the coat was hers?

- **a** — A repair on its sleeve
- **b** — Its size and a new ticket
- **c** — A name written on the seat

**Gabarito editorial:** a. The coat has the same small sleeve repair as Mara's own coat.

**Dicas:** sleeve → manga da roupa; locker → armário individual.

- Continue the story → `announcement`

### A Message Before the Step (`announcement`)

The ceiling speaker clicked. A calm voice said that Mara would step back from the wet seat. She had not moved yet. Then her foot slid backwards across the floor. "You will look at the door now. The door will stay open." Mara looked. The platform door was open, but the red coat was no longer dripping.

**Pergunta:** Why did the first announcement frighten Mara?

- **a** — It warned that another train was arriving
- **b** — It told her the platform door was locked
- **c** — It described her movement before she made it

**Gabarito editorial:** c. The voice described Mara stepping back before she actually moved.

**Dicas:** ceiling → teto; step back → recuou.

- Continue the story → `unwritten-choice`

### A Voice That Waits (`unwritten-choice`)

Her phone camera showed the empty seat. On its screen, the red coat was missing. Mara could photograph the carriage or leave her phone on the seat and walk out. She kept both feet still. "You will choose the picture. You always choose the picture." For the first time, the voice sounded uncertain. The open doorway was only a few steps away.

**Pergunta:** What was different on the phone screen?

- **a** — The doorway was closed
- **b** — The red coat was missing
- **c** — The seat had another passenger

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Mara could see the coat directly, but her camera showed the seat without it.

**Dicas:** uncertain → incerto; sem segurança; doorway → vão da porta.

- Take one photograph and leave → `taken-picture`
- Leave the phone and walk out → `phone-left`

### Evidence on the Platform (`taken-picture`)

Mara took one picture and stepped onto the platform. The train lights went dark behind her. In the photograph, the coat was finally visible, folded neatly on the seat. Her own yellow torch lay beside it. "That cannot be right. I am still holding the torch." She showed the picture to the night manager and refused to return alone. The camera had recorded a scene she had never seen.

**Pergunta:** What made the photograph impossible for Mara?

- **a** — It showed her torch on the seat while she held it
- **b** — It showed daylight on the platform
- **c** — It showed a different colour of coat

**Gabarito editorial:** a. Mara is holding her torch, but the photograph also shows it on the seat.

**Dicas:** neatly → de maneira organizada; refused → recusou.

Fim deste percurso.

### The Call from the Locker (`phone-left`)

Mara left her phone on the seat and walked onto the platform. The speaker went silent. In the staff room, her own red raincoat was still inside her locker. Something vibrated in its pocket. "I left my phone on the train. This pocket should be empty." She closed the locker without reaching inside and called the night manager from the desk phone. Together, they waited for morning outside the room.

**Pergunta:** What does Mara do after she hears the vibration?

- **a** — She reaches into the coat pocket
- **b** — She closes the locker and calls the manager
- **c** — She returns to the train alone

**Gabarito editorial:** b. Mara leaves the pocket untouched, closes the locker and calls from the desk phone.

**Dicas:** vibrated → vibrou; reaching inside → colocar a mão dentro.

Fim deste percurso.
